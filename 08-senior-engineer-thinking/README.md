## Why This Matters

System design isn't just about knowing the components. Senior-level thinking includes:

- **Designing for the future**  -  Not just today's requirements
- **Understanding trade-offs deeply**  -  Every decision has consequences
- **Operating what you build**  -  Observability, incident response
- **Communicating effectively**  -  Architecture reviews, documentation
- **Balancing competing concerns**  -  Cost, performance, reliability, velocity
